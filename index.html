About the game

The Mission

Villages across the land are under siege. As a lone warrior you must cut through every enemy — from Mmawu scouts lurking in the forest to the warlord Zuzugu commanding burning villages. No retreat. No mercy.

Masque Raiders is a side-scrolling mobile action game built in Unity. Progress through multiple levels, each ending with a boss encounter. Defeat all bosses to liberate the land and claim victory.

Stages & Enemies

The Enemy Forces

🌲 Stage 1 — The Forest

Face Mmawu scouts and DarkMmawu warriors across three escalating levels. The forest boss is Entembe.

🔥 Stage 2 — The Villages

Entembe's fallen fighters now join Mmawu as pawns. The warlord Zuzugu commands them all.

⚔️ Wave System

Each level sends randomised waves of enemies. Clear every wave to trigger the boss encounter.

📈 Scaling Difficulty

Enemy speed, health, and aggression increase with each level. Later bosses are formidable.

Combat System

Fight Smart, Fight Hard

👊 Combo Attacks

Chain up to four attacks — Attack1 through Attack4 — with a special combo finisher when the meter is full.

🤸 Flip Attack

Launch a ground flip dealing damage to any enemy in your path. Can only be performed while grounded.

🛡️ Block & Deflect

Hold block to reduce incoming damage. Time it right to deflect projectiles back at enemies.

🦘 Double Jump

Jump twice to avoid attacks and reach elevated positions. A second jump can also be used mid-air.

🔥 Combo Meter

Land hits to fill the combo meter. A full meter unlocks a devastating special attack dealing massive damage.

🏆 Ranking

Earn S, A, B, C, or D rank after each level based on score, time, and whether you took damage.

Rewards

Achievements & Gems

Unlock 16 achievements through combat skill, speed, and survival. Every achievement awards gems that persist across sessions.

💎 Diamond

Awarded for perfect runs, Iron Will, Untouchable, Vanquisher, and Legendary Warrior.

🔴 Ruby

Earned by defeating bosses and landing the first kill.

🟢 Emerald

Rewarded for completing levels and progressing through the story.

🔵 Sapphire

Given for combo mastery, speed runs, and flip attack proficiency.

🟠 Amber

Awarded for survival feats — Last Stand, Shield Wall, and Resilient.

Controls

How to Play

◀ ▶ Movement

On-screen left and right buttons on the left side of the screen.

JUMP

Tap once to jump. Tap again while airborne for a double jump.

ATK — Attack

Tap to attack. Chain taps build a combo. Fill the meter for the combo finisher.

FLIP — Flip Attack

Tap while grounded to perform a forward flip attack dealing damage.

BLK — Block

Hold to block incoming damage and deflect projectiles.

Ncho

Ncho Game Documentation

Everything you need to know to play Ncho — traditional African strategy on your mobile device.

Objective

Capture more stones than your opponent by strategically distributing and collecting stones across the board.

RuleDescription
Starting12 pots × 4 stones each. Player goes first.
Making a moveSelect one of your 6 pots. Stones are distributed clockwise one per pot.
CapturingIf the last stone lands making a pot have exactly 4, you capture all 4.
ContinuingIf the last stone lands in a non-empty pot (not making 4), pick up and continue.
End of turnYour turn ends when the last stone lands in an empty pot.
End of round4 or fewer stones remain. Last capturer takes the remainder.
WinningMost stones wins the round. Win best of 5 rounds to win the game.

💡 Plan Ahead

Think about where your last stone lands before moving.

🎯 Aim for Captures

Land in pots with 3 stones to capture 4.

🛡️ Defensive Play

Avoid leaving pots with 3 stones on your side.

🔄 Chain Moves

Landing in non-empty pots lets you continue — use it strategically!

Ayo2D

Ayo2D Game Documentation

Authentic Yoruba Ayo rules in a beautiful 2D championship format.

Objective

Win 3 out of 5 rounds by capturing more stones than your opponent each round.

RuleDescription
Starting12 pots × 4 stones. Player (blue) goes first.
Making a moveSelect any bottom-row pot. The hand picks up stones and drops them clockwise.
CapturingLast stone landing to make exactly 4 in a pot — capture all 4.
ContinuingLast stone in a non-empty pot (not 4) → pick up and continue.
End of turnLast stone lands in an empty pot.
End of round4 or fewer stones remain. Last capturer takes them.
WinningWin the round by capturing the most stones. Win 3 rounds to win the match.
